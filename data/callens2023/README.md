Tidy tables derived from data accompanying

> Callens M, Rose CJ, Finnegan M, Gatchitch F, Simon L, Hamet J, Pradier L, Dubois M-P, Bedhomme S (2023).
> Hypermutator emergence in experimental *Escherichia coli* populations is stress-type dependent.
> *Evolution Letters* 7(4):252-261. <https://doi.org/10.1093/evlett/qrad019>

Source data are in the public domain (CC0) via Dryad, <https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.hqbzkh1mr>.
Tables here were generated by `pyscript/tidy_callens2023.py`.

- `is10_pcr.csv`: transfer at which IS10 insertion in *mutS* or *mutL* was first detected, and first fixed, per population (from `Timing_of_IS10_insertions_-_PCR_results.xlsx`).
- `populations.csv`: per sequenced population at generation 1,000, counts of detected and fixed mutations and the highest frequency reached by a candidate hypermutator allele (from `mutation_list_g1000.csv`).
- `optical_density.csv`: mean blank-corrected OD600 after 24 h growth, by evolution medium (from `Compiled_OD_measures.csv`).
